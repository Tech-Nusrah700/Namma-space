ABOUT THE PROJECT

Where physical
spaces meet
digital reality.

Namma Space is an immersive 3D space exploration platform that digitally reconstructs real indoor environments — making them accessible, navigable, and interactive, right in a web browser.

We photograph physical spaces, reconstruct them as detailed 3D models, and deliver first-person browser-based exploration — no app installations, no VR headset, no special hardware required.

CAPABILITIES

Built for
real experiences.

🏛

Immersive 3D Visualization

Spaces reconstructed with high-fidelity geometry and realistic lighting, rendered in real-time WebGL.

🕹

Interactive Navigation

Walk freely using intuitive WASD controls and mouse-look — exactly like a first-person experience.

📐

True-Scale Representation

Spaces are reconstructed at real-world scale, preserving accurate proportions, dimensions and depth.

🌐

Browser-Based Access

No plugins or installs. The entire 3D experience runs natively in any modern web browser.

🚀

Orbit Showcase Mode

Auto-rotating preview lets anyone appreciate the space from every angle before exploring first-person.

📱

Responsive Interface

Adapts fluidly across laptops, tablets, and desktops with a consistent, polished UI at every size.

PROCESS

How it
comes alive.

01
📸

Capture

Photograph the physical indoor space from multiple angles to capture complete spatial information.

02
⚙️

Reconstruct

Process images to generate a detailed 3D model with accurate geometry, surfaces, and materials.

03
📦

Load

Export the model as a .glb file and load it into the web-based 3D viewer for rendering.

04
🌍

Explore

Navigate the reconstructed space freely in your browser — walk around, look anywhere, discover every corner.
